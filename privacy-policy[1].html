Stint privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Stint is a focus timer for Chrome. This policy explains what Stint records, where it is kept, and what control you have over it.

What Stint records

Only while a session timer you started is running, Stint records:

  • The web address (domain) and page title of the tab in front of you
  • When you are idle, away from Chrome, or switching between sites
  • The goal you type, your session length, scores and goal check-ins
  • Files, images and links you choose to attach to a session
  • Sessions you book in the timetable, your groups, and the curated sessions you create (their names, times, websites and expected files)
  • If you install the optional Windows desktop helper: the name and window title of the app in front of you

Stint does not track your browsing when no session is running. It does not read the content of web pages, form fields, passwords or keystrokes.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored locally in your browser on your own computer. Stint has no servers and no accounts. Your data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and Stint contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Sharing and selling

Stint does not sell, share, rent or transfer your data to anyone, for any reason.

Your control

  • Export your sessions and settings at any time from History and settings, Export as JSON.
  • Delete individual sessions, or all of them, from the same page.
  • Uninstalling Stint removes all of its data from your browser.
